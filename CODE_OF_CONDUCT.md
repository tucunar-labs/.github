# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Tucunar Labs projects to be a respectful experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect privacy: do not publish other people's personal data, credentials or
  private conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention,
sustained disruption and publishing private information without permission are
not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report incidents to **hola@tucunar.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban participants depending on the
severity and repetition of the behavior.

This policy is based on the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
